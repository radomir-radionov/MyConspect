## DDD Fundamentals
What is _Domain-Driven Design (DDD)_ and what are its _core principles_?
How does _DDD_ differ from traditional _data-driven_ or _service-driven_ approaches?
What is the difference between the _Domain Model_ and the _Data Model_ in _DDD_?
Can you explain what _Bounded Contexts_ are in _DDD_, and why they are important?
What strategies can you use to define _Bounded Contexts_ in a large system?
How do you integrate multiple _Bounded Contexts_ within a single system?
Why is _Ubiquitous Language_ important in _DDD_, and how do you establish it?
What is the role of _Entities_ in _DDD_, and how do they differ from _Value Objects_?
How would you identify _Aggregates_ in a domain model, and what _boundary rules_ apply to them?
Can you explain what a _Domain Event_ is and give an example of when it might be used?

## Tactical Design in DDD
How do _Repositories_ function in _DDD_ and what is their main responsibility?
What is the difference between a _Repository_ and a _Service_ in _DDD_?
How would you handle complex domain logic that involves multiple _entities_ and _value objects_?
Can you delineate the role of a _Domain Service_ versus an _Application Service_?
What considerations are there for implementing _Aggregates_ to ensure _transactional consistency_?
How do you deal with performance issues when loading large _Aggregates_?
In what scenarios might you use a _Specification pattern_ in _DDD_?
What are some Domain Modeling strategies to enforce _invariants_ within an _Aggregate_?
Can you discuss strategies for dealing with _concurrency_ and _synchronization_ within _Aggregates_?

## Domain Events and Event Sourcing
How can _Domain Events_ help in designing a _loosely coupled system_?
What is _Event Sourcing_, and how does it differ from standard _persistence_ mechanisms?
Can you explain how _CQRS (Command Query Responsibility Segregation)_ complements _DDD_?
In what situations would you choose to implement _Event Sourcing_ in a _DDD_ context?
How do you typically _handle events_ that should trigger _side effects_ in other _Bounded Contexts_?

## Strategic Design and DDD Implementation
How does _DDD_ address the issue of _complex domain logic_ changes over time?
Describe the concept of a _context map_ in _DDD_ and its purpose.
What is the _Anti-Corruption Layer_ in _DDD_, and when would you use one?
How might you implement a _Policy_ in _DDD_, and what are its advantages?
Can you give an example of how _DDD_ can coexist with _microservices architecture_?
What factors would influence the decision to refactor a legacy system with a _DDD_ approach?

## Advanced DDD Concepts
Explain how _DDD principles_ can be applied at different scales in a software project.
How can _DDD_ help in understanding and aligning complex organizational structures?
How does _DDD_ assist in creating a _pluggable component architecture_?
How does _DDD_ help manage distributed systems complexity, particularly in _consistency management_?
What are the challenges involved in implementing _DDD_ in a large, multidisciplinary team?

## Practical Considerations
What are _common pitfalls_ when transitioning an existing project to _DDD_?
How do you measure the success of _DDD implementation_ in a project?
What tools or _frameworks_ are supportive of _DDD_ and why?
How would you address the training and knowledge transfer needed for a team new to _DDD_?
Can you provide an example of a project where _DDD_ provided clear benefits over other design approaches?
